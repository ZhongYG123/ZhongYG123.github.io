Common Questions

What is the MOQ?

Our minimum order quantity is 1 set for sample/trial orders. For bulk pricing, we recommend orders of 10+ sets.

How long does shipping take?

Sea freight to Africa typically takes 25–40 days. Air freight is 5–10 days. We'll advise the best option based on your location and urgency.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept T/T (bank transfer), Western Union, and L/C (Letter of Credit). Payment terms: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment.

Do you offer warranty?

Yes. All FoolProof® roller assemblies come with a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Extended warranty options available.
