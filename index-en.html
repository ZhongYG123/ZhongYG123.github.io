ABOUT US

Three Decades of Engineering Excellence

Zhengzhou Chenxinghe Trading Co., Ltd. stands at the crossroads of China's mining machinery heartland — Zhengzhou, Henan. With roots stretching back more than 30 years, we have grown into a comprehensive heavy equipment enterprise serving clients across 180+ countries and regions.

Our expertise spans four core sectors: construction aggregates, mining crushing, metallic mineral processing, and green building materials. From jaw crushers and cone crushers to Raymond mills, VSI sand makers, and our flagship FoolProof® Oil Assembly — 40+ product categories cover the entire mineral processing chain.

We operate an ISO-aligned quality management system backed by QC circles, SPC statistical process control, 6S workplace management, and rigorous audit mechanisms.
