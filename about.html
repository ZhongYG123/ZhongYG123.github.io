Our Story

From Zhengzhou to the World

Zhengzhou Chenxinghe Trading Co., Ltd. was founded in Zhengzhou, Henan Province — the heart of China's mining equipment manufacturing hub. With our own factory and a dedicated engineering team, we specialize in the design, manufacture, and export of oil-lubricated roller assemblies for Raymond mills.

We saw a persistent problem in the industry: conventional grease-lubricated rollers require frequent maintenance, suffer from premature bearing failure, and cause costly downtime for mill operators. Our response was FoolProof® — a sealed oil-lubrication system that dramatically extends service life and reduces total cost of ownership.

Today, we supply mills across Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East, with a monthly production capacity of 400 roller assemblies and a growing reputation for reliability and value.

Our Facility

Manufacturing Excellence

Our factory in Zhengzhou is equipped with modern CNC machining centers, assembly lines, and a dedicated quality control laboratory.

Precision Machining

CNC lathes, milling machines, and grinding equipment ensure every component meets exact dimensional tolerances. Our machining capabilities cover the full manufacturing chain — from raw material cutting to final surface finishing.

Assembly & Testing

Each roller assembly is built on dedicated assembly stations by experienced technicians. Every unit undergoes pressure testing for seal integrity and rotational testing for bearing smoothness before leaving the factory.

Quality Control

Incoming material inspection, in-process checks, and final QC — our three-stage quality system ensures consistency. We maintain detailed production records for every batch, enabling full traceability.

Packaging & Logistics

Export-grade wooden crates with rust-prevention treatment. We coordinate sea freight, air freight, and door-to-door delivery. Documentation support includes commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin.
